Cyclone Akvan: Over One Lakh Evacuated in Odisha, Red Alert Issued for Three Andhra Pradesh Districts

Authorities in eastern India have initiated massive preparations and evacuations as Severe Cyclonic Storm 'Akvan' approaches the coast. In Odisha, over one lakh people—including nearly 10,000 pregnant women—have been shifted to safer locations ahead of the storm, which is expected to make landfall near Gopalpur on Friday, November 20, 2026. The Odisha government has also positioned disaster response forces, emptied reservoirs in southern and coastal districts, and closed major tourist sites such as Chilika Lake, Puri beaches, and the Nandankanan Zoological Park.

Meanwhile, neighboring Andhra Pradesh remains on high alert, with red alerts issued for three districts. Districts such as Srikakulam and Vizianagaram are taking precautionary measures, with officials reviewing preparedness in vulnerable border areas like Ichchapuram. Srikakulam is bracing for another severe weather event after suffering heavily from two recent cyclones, Arnab and Murasu, earlier in the season. Local authorities have ordered fishermen not to venture into the sea.

In addition to Odisha and Andhra Pradesh, the India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast heavy rain and thunderstorms with gusty winds reaching 30-40 kmph for southern districts of West Bengal, including Kolkata, through Saturday. Coastal areas in West Bengal have also been advised to keep fishermen out of the rough waters of the Bay of Bengal as the storm system progresses.

UK Inflation Rises to 3.6% Amid Warnings of Significant Energy Bill Increases

UK inflation rose to 3.6% in the year to October, up from 3.5% in September, marking its highest level in a year. According to official figures from the Office for National Statistics (ONS), the increase was primarily driven by higher petrol and diesel prices, as well as broader pressures stemming from the ongoing conflict in the Middle East affecting global energy costs. Fuel prices for motorists saw a significant increase, with diesel reaching record highs in data dating back to 1996.

Simultaneously, energy consultancy Cornwall Insight has forecast that typical household gas and electricity bills could jump by 26% starting in January, adding £443 a year to reach an average of £2,166. In response to mounting inflationary pressures, the Bank of England recently raised interest rates from 3.75% to 4% and warned that additional borrowing cost increases could follow if the conflict-driven energy price pressures persist.

To help alleviate the burden on consumers, the government has implemented measures including zero VAT on electricity bills until next July and a planned £100 payment for millions of households. However, analysts and energy experts note that while these measures offer some relief, they only partially offset the heavy financial impact of soaring energy forecasts and living costs.

Waymo Suspends Driverless Taxi Operations on U.S. Freeways Following California Ban

Alphabet subsidiary Waymo has temporarily suspended its driverless taxi operations on freeways across the United States. The nationwide restriction follows a decision by the California Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) to ban Waymo's driverless vehicles from state freeways, citing an "imminent hazard to public safety" stemming from recent traffic incidents.

According to state officials, the regulatory action was prompted by events in early November. In one incident, three driverless Waymo vehicles entered an active construction zone on US Route 101 south of San Francisco where workers and equipment were present. In another widely circulated social media incident, a Waymo vehicle attempted to overtake California Highway Patrol vehicles that were deliberately slowing traffic. State officials noted that similar construction zone incursions had also occurred previously in May.

Following the California ban, Waymo chose to implement a voluntary nationwide suspension of commercial freeway operations in all markets where it offers the service, including the San Francisco Bay Area, Los Angeles, Phoenix, Miami, and Austin, while continuing testing with safety drivers. The company stated that no injuries or collisions resulted from the construction zone encounters. While freeway rides are halted, Waymo's driverless taxis are permitted to continue operating on regular city streets in its operational cities.

California's restrictions will remain in place until Waymo demonstrates that it has taken effective corrective actions to address the identified safety deficiencies. Waymo indicated it is working diligently and responsibly with state officials to resolve the issues and eventually resume freeway operations.

Launched in September 1977, the probe originally photographed Jupiter and Saturn before continuing its journey outward. In 2012, it crossed the heliopause to enter interstellar space, becoming the farthest human-made object from Earth.

At its current distance, radio signals traveling at the speed of light now require 24 hours to reach the spacecraft and another 24 hours for the return signal to arrive back on Earth. The probe carries a gold-plated copper disk designed by a committee chaired by astronomer Carl Sagan. Commentators note that the mission not only expands scientific understanding of the solar system's outer boundaries and interstellar space, but also serves as a reflective vantage point for humanity.
