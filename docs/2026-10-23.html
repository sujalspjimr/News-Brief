Magnitude 7.0 Earthquake Strikes Tibet, Causing Casualties and Infrastructure Damage

A powerful magnitude 7.0 earthquake struck a mountainous, high-altitude region of Tibet late Thursday night, resulting in at least 16 deaths and dozens of injuries. The epicentre hit Baqên County in the Nagqu region, sending residents fleeing outdoors into subzero temperatures. According to reports citing Chinese state media, hundreds of homes and livestock sheds collapsed, electricity supplies were temporarily disrupted, and rockfalls closed sections of local roads, hindering rescue efforts in the remote terrain.

Emergency services, police officers, and thousands of personnel were deployed to the affected zone to conduct search and rescue operations, clear debris, and assist with relocations. Chinese authorities declared an emergency, with Premier Li Qiang and President Xi Jinping calling for urgent relief efforts, medical treatment, and proper resettlement for those impacted. The government allocated 50 million yuan for disaster relief while sending supplies such as prefabricated housing units, tents, and winter clothing.

Survivors recounted harrowing escapes as the initial tremor struck, followed by hundreds of aftershocks registered in the area. The high altitude of approximately 4,800 meters, combined with freezing temperatures and expected intermittent snow, presented significant challenges for emergency responders working to reach isolated victims and clear blocked highways.

Africa Faces Complex Ebola Outbreak Following Spread to Nigeria

Health officials and international organizations have warned that Africa is facing a complex Ebola outbreak after the virus spread from East Africa to Nigeria, marking Nigeria's first confirmed case in more than a decade. The World Health Organization noted the added complexity as the virus reached the western part of the continent, following cases and deaths in the Democratic Republic of Congo, Uganda, and Kenya.

The confirmed patient in Nigeria is a 42-year-old Ugandan-born woman who is the wife of a Swiss diplomat. She spent time in Uganda before returning to Nigeria on October 3, and later tested positive for Ebola on October 20 after spending about a week at a private clinic in Abuja with symptoms. Authorities are currently investigating whether she may have crossed paths at a Ugandan airport with a Kenyan man who also travelled through Uganda and subsequently died from the virus in Kenya.

While health authorities continue to trace contacts and investigate potential transmission points, responses have involved coordination between national ministries, the World Health Organization, and the Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention. In the Democratic Republic of Congo, churches and community leaders have also played a role in assisting health care workers by addressing fear and misinformation during efforts to control the outbreak.

Pankaj Advani Wins 29th World Title at World Billiards Championship

The summit match featured shifting momentum and high-quality play from both competitors. Hall built a substantial lead of over 300 points, reaching a score of 1014 to 697 with less than two hours remaining on the clock, placing him in a strong position for what would have been his maiden world title after five previous runner-up finishes. However, Advani mounted a dramatic comeback, responding with a fluent 210 break followed by a mammoth 587 break—the highest of the tournament—to seal the victory.

En route to the final, Advani defeated compatriot Sourav Kothari in the quarterfinals and overcame David Causier in the semi-finals. Following his success in Birmingham, Advani is set to transition disciplines to prepare for the IBSF World Snooker Championship scheduled for mid-November in Indore.
